# Community conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Welcome questions, give constructive
feedback, and discuss ideas without personal attacks. Harassment, discrimination,
threats, sexualized attention, and publishing another person's private information
are unacceptable in project spaces.

Maintainers may remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation to
protect the community. Apply these expectations fairly, including to maintainers.

Report concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile to arrange
a confidential channel. Do not include private information in a public issue.
If the concern involves a maintainer, contact another maintainer where available.
